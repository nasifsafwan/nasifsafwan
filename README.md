### Hey, I'm Nasif.

AI researcher and developer out of Dhaka. CSE graduate, AIUB.

I co-founded **[Arché Intelligence Lab](https://www.archelab.org)** and direct its research: 35+ people across a few countries, teaching machines to read brain scans and working out how brains actually think. By day I ship production AI at **[Ledgercross](https://www.ledgercross.com/team/nasif-safwan)**. On the side I keep **[FrithX](https://frithx.com)** running.
